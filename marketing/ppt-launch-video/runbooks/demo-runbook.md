# Demo Runbook

## Deterministic generation flow

1. Start from a clean English OfficeDex workspace.
2. Select PPTX.
3. Submit `copy/demo-prompt.txt` exactly.
4. Confirm the representative Story Beats node.
5. Select `Approve Story Beats`.
6. Confirm the representative Slide Outlines node.
7. Select `Approve Slide Outlines`.
8. Confirm the representative Generated Slides node.
9. Select `Approve Generated Slides`.
10. Wait for the real nine-slide PPTX to load in PPTist.

## Edit flow

1. Open slide 6, the 90-day launch timeline.
2. Enter `Turn this launch timeline into a vertical roadmap.`
3. Review the proposed edit.
4. Apply the edit.
5. Confirm that the final slide is vertical and the rest of the deck remains intact.

## Recording acceptance

- No onboarding overlay, update banner, generic question composer, Chinese text, private path, or account information appears.
- All confirmation actions happen through the visible OfficeDex interface.
- The preview uses the real OfficeCLI-generated PPTX.
- The before and after timeline remain legible at 1920×1080.
